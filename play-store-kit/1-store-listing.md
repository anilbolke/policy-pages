## English (en-IN), default language

**Short description** (80 max)

```
Daily, home & trip expenses, udhaar khata and bill reminders. Works offline.
```

**Full description** (4000 max)

```
Kharcha Khata is a simple, private money diary for every Indian household. Track daily spending, run the home budget, split trip costs with friends, keep an udhaar khata and never miss a bill. Everything stays on your phone. No sign-up, no ads, no internet needed.

DAILY & HOME EXPENSES
• Add an expense in seconds: amount, category, note, date
• Separate Daily and Home books with monthly budgets and 80% / 100% alerts
• Indian categories built in: kirana, milk, sabzi, petrol, auto, recharge, rent/EMI, maid, school fees and more
• Choose "Other" and type your own category
• Attach a photo of the bill to any expense

SPEAK TO ADD
• Tap the mic and say "Milk 60 and sabzi 200" or "Yesterday auto 80"
• Works in English, Hindi and Marathi
• Several expenses in one sentence, checked before saving

TRIPS WITH FRIENDS
• Add members and record who paid
• Split equally, by custom amounts, or mark an expense as one person's own
• Settle up shows the fewest payments needed; tap "Mark paid" when done
• Trip budget with spend per member

UDHAAR KHATA
• "You gave" and "You got" for every person, with a running balance
• Record part payments
• Pick people from your contacts and set a "remind me to collect / return" date
• Send a polite reminder or the full statement on WhatsApp

BILLS & EMIs
• Rent, EMI, Netflix, insurance, school fees: monthly, quarterly or yearly
• Paid / Skip on the due date, or record automatically
• Phone reminder a day before

INCOME & SAVINGS
• Add salary, business or other income
• See Income, Spent and Saved for every month

REPORTS & EXPORT
• Day-by-day and month-by-month charts, category breakdown
• Export to Excel (CSV) or a clean PDF report

FOR EVERYONE
• Simple mode: just daily spending with big buttons
• Family mode: home, trips, budgets and income
• Advanced mode: adds udhaar khata
• Turn any section on or off in Settings

PRIVATE & SECURE
• Your data stays on your phone; we never see it
• PIN and fingerprint / face lock
• One-file backup and restore, including bill photos
• Dark mode

Made in Nanded, Maharashtra by RANA.AI Technology. Questions or ideas? Write to ranaaitechnology@gmail.com.
```
