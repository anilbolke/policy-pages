Kharcha Khata Privacy Policy

App by RANA.AI Technology · Last updated 13 November 2026

In short

  • Everything you enter in Kharcha Khata stays on your phone.
  • The app has no sign-up, no account and no server. We (RANA.AI Technology) do not receive, collect, sell or share your data.
  • There are no ads and no analytics or tracking tools in the app.
  • Contacts, microphone, notifications and fingerprint are used only when you choose a feature that needs them.

What the app stores, and where

The app saves the following only in the app's private storage on your phone:

  • Expenses, income, budgets, trips and trip members, bills and EMIs, and udhaar (lend and borrow) entries that you type or speak.
  • Bill photos you choose to attach.
  • For udhaar: the name, and if you add it, the mobile number of each person you record.
  • Your settings, including a scrambled (hashed) form of your app PIN. The PIN itself is never stored.

None of this is sent to RANA.AI Technology or anyone else. If you uninstall the app or erase its data, it is deleted from the phone. Android's own device backup, if you have it turned on, may include app data in your personal Google backup under Google's terms; we have no access to it.

Permissions and why they are used

PermissionWhen it is usedWhat happens to the data
Contacts (read)Only when you tap Contacts in Udhaar Khata to pick a person.Only the name and number you pick are saved, on your phone. The app does not read or upload your contact list.
MicrophoneOnly while you use voice entry (the mic button).Your phone's speech service (for example, Google speech recognition) turns your words into text. The app receives only the text, does not record or keep audio, and sends nothing to us.
NotificationsFor bill, EMI and udhaar reminders you set.Reminders are scheduled on the phone.
Fingerprint / face (biometric)Only if you turn on app lock with fingerprint or face.Checked by Android. The app never receives your fingerprint or face data.
Photos and filesWhen you attach a bill photo or restore a backup.Only the file you pick is used, and it stays on your phone.
InternetOnly when you open a link yourself, such as WhatsApp or our website.The app does not upload your data.

Sharing that you start

When you tap Share, Send reminder, WhatsApp, Export CSV/PDF or Backup, the app prepares a message or file and hands it to the app you choose (for example WhatsApp, Gmail or Google Drive). What happens next is under that app's privacy policy. Shared reminder messages include a short line saying they were sent from Kharcha Khata.

Children

The app is meant for adults managing their own money and is not directed at children under 13.

Security

Your data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. You can add a PIN or fingerprint lock. The lock stops other people from opening the app; it does not encrypt the stored data, so please also keep your phone locked and your backup files safe.

Your choices

  • Edit or delete any entry at any time.
  • Erase everything from Settings › Erase all data, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Turn off any permission in your phone's Settings › Apps › Kharcha Khata. The rest of the app keeps working.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and the date above before the change reaches the app.
